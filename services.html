Core Transport

Our standard services

01

Ambulatory Transport

For passengers who can walk with little or no assistance.

  • Door-to-door assistance
  • Clean, comfortable vehicles
  • Appointments, dialysis, therapy
02

Wheelchair Transport

Lift-equipped vans with full securement for wheelchair users.

  • Hydraulic lifts & ramps
  • 4-point securement systems
  • Trained attendants
03

Gurney Transport

Stretcher-level transport for passengers who must lie flat.

  • Bed-level comfort & stability
  • Secure gurney locking
  • Facility & hospital discharge
Specialty Transport

Specialized services

Additional care options for passengers with specific needs.

Bariatric Transport

Reinforced equipment and trained staff to safely and respectfully transport heavier or larger passengers.

Oxygen-Tank Transport

Safe accommodation and securement of portable oxygen for passengers who require supplemental O₂ during transit.

Long-Distance Transport

Comfortable point-to-point trips beyond our standard service area — including out-of-county and out-of-region transport.
