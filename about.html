Who We Are

Care in motion

Every day, people across Northern California need a safe, dependable way to get to medical appointments, treatments, and back home. Guardian Medical Services exists to make that journey easy, comfortable, and worry-free.

We're a locally rooted non-emergency medical transportation provider serving eleven counties across the Sacramento Valley and Northern California. Our drivers aren't just operators — they're certified caregivers who treat every passenger with the dignity and patience they deserve.

From a single wheelchair ride to a long-distance transfer, we show up on time, equipped, and ready to help.

At a glance

  • Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Serving 11 NorCal counties
  • HIPAA, First Aid & CPR certified drivers
  • Ambulatory, wheelchair, gurney & specialty fleet
