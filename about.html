Your trusted partner in patient transportation

Guardian Medical Services was built on a simple belief: getting to care should never be the hard part.

Who we are

Care in motion

Every day, people across Northern California need a safe, dependable way to get to medical appointments, treatments, and back home. Guardian Medical Services exists to make that trip easy, comfortable, and worry-free.

We're a locally rooted non-emergency medical transportation provider serving eleven counties across the Sacramento Valley and Northern California. Our drivers aren't just operators. They're certified caregivers who treat every passenger with dignity and patience.

From a single wheelchair ride to a long-distance transfer, we show up on time, equipped, and ready to help.

Our values

What drives every ride

Safety first

Inspected vehicles, certified staff, and careful procedures on every trip. Nothing matters more than getting you there safely.

Compassion

We treat every passenger like family, with patience, kindness, and respect for their independence and dignity.

Reliability
